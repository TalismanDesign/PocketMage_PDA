Keyboard:

Screen:
- Changed all 4.7uF caps to 0805
- MPR121 on the screen PCB shares the I2C_SDA_SWITCHED and I2C_SCL_SWITCHED bus from the keyboard PCB. 10k pull-ups exist for these I2C pins on the keyboard PCB and the screen PCB. The total pull-up on each line is 5k, which is acceptable for I2C (4.7k is a standard pull-up). Pullups on this switched I2C line are switched off in sleep mode, so current draw is not a significant problem.
